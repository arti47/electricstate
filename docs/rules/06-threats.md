# Threats

A Threat is a person, group or phenomenon that can harm NPCs or Travelers, or block the Journey. It can exist in the real world, in a neuroscape, or both. A Stop may hold several, but one is usually the main adversary, and the Stop Countdown should be driven by it.

## Anatomy
- **Location** — home turf, defining the Threat and often hosting the final confrontation.
- **Goal** — specific, obsessive. Goals aimed at the Stop and its NPCs proceed with or without the Travelers ("what will you do about it?"); Goals aimed at the Travelers are more direct but leave less freedom, better for inexperienced players.
- **Reaction** — how it responds on first contact with the Travelers.
- **Characteristics** — attributes, talents and gear as per Chapters 3–4, sometimes with special gear or abilities. NPCs never push.
- **Health** — Incapacitated as normal, but no death rolls: the GM decides whether it dies. Threats have no Hope.
- Threats are meant to be recycled — change stats, Countdown steps, or Goal.

Never point an unbeatable Threat directly at the Travelers; let it harm others, or even court them, and let their actions decide whether it counts them as friend or enemy.

## Categories and examples
- **Violent** — law enforcement, secret agents, gang members, crazed killers. (Sample stats: Law Enforcement Str 4 Agi 4 Wit 3 Emp 3, Health 4, Pistoleer; Secret Agent Str 4 Agi 5 Wit 3 Emp 2, Health 5, Stealthy; Gang Member Str 3 Agi 4 Wit 3 Emp 3, Health 4; Crazed Killer Str 5 Agi 5 Wit 3 Emp 4, Health 5.)
- **Manipulative** — cultists, local strongmen, business leaders.
- **Technological** — robots (drone stats, no Wits/Empathy, no talents; sample Str 6 Agi 4 Hull 5 Armor 6), drone growths, systems, Intercerebral Intelligences, and Sentre itself. Usually tied to a human creator or would-be controller, and often confronted inside a neuroscape as well as outside. Special abilities menu: flight, high Hull, Armor, built-in weapons (no gear bonus), entering neuroscapes without a neurocaster, driving avatars, drawing on networked collective knowledge.
- **Environmental** — extreme weather, earthquakes, floods, wildfire, animals, disease. No stats of their own; they apply Chapter 4 hazards. Weather has no motive, so tension comes from survival plus trapped-together Traveler Tension.
- **Past sins** — unfinished business returning from an earlier Stop.
- **Personal Threats** — one per Traveler, the most effective engagement tool. Countdown steps are independent of Stops and can fire on the road; each step closes the distance (heard about → contact → attack). The Traveler's beliefs about the Threat may be wrong, or the truth may be worse. A personal Threat can be hooked onto a Stop Threat. Either side may declare a personal Threat played out; the player then invents a new one or continues without.

Every Threat category in the book carries a 4-step example Countdown, usable as a template.

## Minor NPCs
Everyone who isn't a Threat: victims, petitioners, helpers.

**Baseline stat rule (p. 151):** every minor NPC is assumed to have **3 in all attributes**
unless stated otherwise, and may have one talent indicating a specific proficiency. They are
described with a name and a function ("Valeria Rocca, Mayor of Hartville") plus an optional
quirk — see the D66 quirks table in [12-generator-tables.md](12-generator-tables.md).

**Ending a personal Threat (p. 150):** either the GM or the player may declare at any time that
a personal Threat has played itself out. The player then invents a new one or continues without.
Ending a personal Threat is a victory that may grow the Traveler — or reveal the Threat was the
tip of something larger.
