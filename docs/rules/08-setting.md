# Setting — Pacifica, 1997

Alternate late '90s: the culture of the decade intact, the United States shattered by a second civil war, and neuronics eating what's left.

## Elevator pitch (the book's own bullets)
- Alternate 1990s with the decade's iconic artifacts and styles.
- The USA was torn apart by civil war; play starts in **Pacifica**, formerly California.
- The war was bound up with **neuronics** — technology that mimics the human brain.
- **Neurocasters** (plastic-cased helmets) put a mind inside a drone, or into an avatar in a **neuroscape** — hyperreal VR.
- Whoever controls neuronics controls the world; foremost **Sentre**, maker of neurocasters and builder of neurograph towers across Pacifica.
- The world is falling apart by neglect, not by explosion. No going back.
- Something may be alive inside the network, using human minds and bodies.
- Creatures of metal and meat roam the land ("neuronic ghosts", phantasms); human monsters — militias, cults, looters — are certain.
- The coastal cities still function; the collapse advances from the east.

## Timeline
- Divergence in the early '70s: economic crisis, civil rights, Watergate, Vietnam (bloodier for drone-ship bombing). Ford's pardon of Nixon triggers nationwide protest; the president is wounded by a shooter, and anti-war activist Lucca Rossi is arrested. Riots, assassinations, the storming of the Capitol, and the **Franklin Square massacre** ignite militia war.
- The union fragments into new nations. In the west the **Pacific Congregation** forms, then collapses.
- Drone warfare peaks; drone ships bombard whole states. The war ends leaving wrecks across the landscape.
- **1996 — Mode 6**: Sentre's sixth major system upgrade. Something changes in the network; theories range from an emergent AI harvesting human brains as distributed processing, to an **Intercerebral Intelligence**, to foreign infiltration. Users don't care — the neuroscape becomes overwhelmingly addictive, and roamers wander wired to hulking drones until they die.
- **1997** — play begins.

## Pacifica
Formerly California. A police state under President Griffin, effectively steered by corporations. Functional on the coast, dying inland; drought and dust swallow towns in the southeast. Landmarks include Memorial City, New Eylar, Los Angeles, Sacramento, and the drone-ship graveyards.

**San Francisco** — the freest city: few neuro towers, the lowest addiction rate, an intact library system (Main Library, 100 Larkin St.), free-thinkers and artists, Silicon Valley's neuro engineers to the south, and the Sacramento River delta wetland communities. Its mayor, Jon Phu, is rumoured to be President Griffin's lover, relative, twin — or a corporate-built drone.

## Corporations
- **Sentre** — dominant multinational; neurocasters, drones, robots, games, neuronics, micro drones. Nominally run by Valeria Lopez, unseen for years; rumour says something else is in control. Rarely intervenes except to spread the network.
- **Happy-Cool** — neurocasters and neuroscapes for children, billboards of a smiling yellow head; expansion planned toward the elderly, the marginalized, and pets.
- **LifeBuddy** — assisted death; two signatures suffice for the elderly, neither needing to be the subject's. Avatars in neuroscapes claim to have been "helped into the afterlife".
- **Grizzly Burger** — food supplier trading on a fabricated founding myth.

## Other nations
- **Northern Union** (Washington, Oregon; capital Seattle) — fragile democracy, close trading partner, no passports needed. Relations strained over state-funded labs seeking a cure for neuro addiction.
- **Blackwelt Exclusion Zone** (Nevada) — desert and ruins after the war's most destructive drone bombardments; Reno, Henderson, Boulder City and parts of Vegas survive; rumours of underground military bases and a refurbished-luxury-goods black market.
- **Libertania** (Utah, Colorado, Arizona, New Mexico) — safest and most prosperous; guards its southern border; hosts the Addison Art Retreat in Santa Fe, where art is made inside the network.
- **South Republic** (Texas, Louisiana, Oklahoma and parts nearby) — claims to be the true remnant of the United States and recognizes no other nation.

## Neuronic phenomena
- **Neurograph towers** and neurocaster terminals; wired access is stronger than wireless.
- **Neuroscapes** — from paradises to "darkscapes"; local or global.
- **Intercerebral Intelligences** — entities born or grown inside the network, with Wits and Empathy potentially beyond human range, rumoured to alter human genomes.
- **The Convergence** — the oldest techno cult, founded by retired drone pilots who felt a presence in the network; many suffered stillbirths. Worships the Inter Cerebral Deity and seeks the Rapture. Seen as zombie-like hordes with glowing neurocasters, black-wired to enormous scrap drones.
- **Drone growths** — huge jury-rigged drones, sometimes wired to people, possibly the physical bodies of Intercerebral Intelligences; act in both realms.
- **Neurine** — the neuro-drug of the setting; **dream glint** is extracted from wrecked neuronics.
- Everything else is ordinary '90s low-tech: Walkmans, VHS, phone booths, film cameras.
