# Injury & Trauma Tables

Transcribed from source page images (pp. 84, 87). These confirm the transcript's own rendering
of both tables — the transcript is reliable here.

## Serious Injuries (D66, p. 84)

Rolled after being Incapacitated and surviving.

| D66 | Injury | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 11–36 | None | — |
| 41 | Broken finger | –1 die on all rolls that normally require two arms (rifle, driving, climbing). Healing: D6 days. |
| 42 | Ear torn off | –1 die on Wits rolls for hearing. Healing: 2D6 days. |
| 43 | Broken toe | Move **or** act in a round, not both. Healing: D6 days. |
| 44 | Broken ribs | –2 dice on Strength and Agility. Healing: D6 days. |
| 45 | Teeth knocked out | –1 die on Empathy. Healing: 2D6 days. |
| 46 | Concussion | –2 dice on Wits. Healing: D6 days. |
| 51 | Broken nose | –1 die on Empathy. Healing: D6 days. |
| 52 | Busted knee | Move **or** act in a round, not both. Healing: 2D6 days. |
| 53 | Traumatized | Roll once on the Mental Trauma table. |
| 54 | Gouged eye | –2 dice on Wits rolls for spotting. Healing: D6 days. |
| 55 | Damaged throat | –2 dice on Empathy. Healing: D6 days. |
| 56 | Infected wound | Afflicted by a disease of Virulence 6. |
| 61 | Broken arm | –3 dice on all rolls requiring two arms. Healing: 3D6 days. |
| 62 | Broken leg | Move **or** act in a round, not both. Healing: 3D6 days. |
| 63 | Disfigured face | –2 dice on Empathy. **Requires surgery.** Healing: 2D6 days. |
| 64 | Punctured lung | –2 dice on Strength and Agility. **Requires surgery.** Healing: 2D6 days. |
| 65 | Cracked skull | –2 dice on Wits. **Requires surgery.** Healing: 2D6 days. |
| 66 | Internal bleeding | –2 dice on Strength and Agility, and every such roll inflicts D6 damage. **Requires surgery.** Healing: 2D6 days. |

## Mental Trauma (D66, p. 87)

Rolled after a Breakdown that was rallied. One Wits **or** Empathy roll per week to recover.
The book prefaces this table with a group-consent note; the app surfaces it behind the same
warning.

| D66 | Trauma | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 11–36 | None | — |
| 41 | Confused | –1 die on Wits rolls. |
| 42 | Apathetic | Cannot push any dice rolls. |
| 43 | Reclusive | Cannot recover Hope by reducing Tension. |
| 44 | Obsessive | Must push every dice roll — even a successful one. |
| 45 | Overwhelmed | –2 dice on all rolls to resist traumatic events. |
| 46 | Alcoholic | Can only recover Hope by drinking a dose of alcohol, max 1 point per day. |
| 51 | Depressed | Cannot be rallied by another person during a Breakdown. |
| 52 | Worrisome | Minor concerns (GM's choice) count as traumatic events, potential Hope loss 1. |
| 53 | Changed personality | Randomly generate a new Dream. |
| 54 | Phobic | Phobia tied to the trauma (GM's choice); every contact is a traumatic event, potential Hope loss 2. |
| 55 | Speech loss | Cannot speak; communicate by notes or signs. |
| 56 | Nightmares | Every Shift slept is a traumatic event, potential Hope loss 1. |
| 61 | Flashbacks | Potential Hope loss from all traumatic events increased by 1. |
| 62 | Panic attacks | Losing 1+ Hope to a traumatic event triggers an automatic Breakdown. |
| 63 | Violent | Losing 1+ Hope to a traumatic event makes you attack the nearest person in close combat instead of freezing; fight until you take damage. |
| 64 | Psychotic | Once every Shift (GM's choice), hallucinations count as traumatic events, potential Hope loss 1. |
| 65 | Amnesiac | Cannot remember who you or the other player characters are; roleplayed. |
| 66 | Personality split | Create a second personality: different name, Wits and Empathy, a Dream, a random talent, separate Tension scores from zero. Switch every time you lose a point of Hope for any reason. |

**Automation note.** Entries 42, 44, 45, 46, 51, 52, 54, 56, 61, 62, 63, 64 and 66 rewrite dice
or economy rules and must be enforced by the engine, not merely displayed. 44 (must push) and
42 (cannot push) are directly contradictory if both are somehow held — the engine resolves in
favour of the more recent trauma and warns.
