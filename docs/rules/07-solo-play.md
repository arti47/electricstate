# Solo / GM-less Play (The Lone Traveler)

Tools: D6s plus a 52-card deck (no jokers). You run 2–4 Travelers, spotlighting one "main character" per Stop and rotating so each leads at least one Stop.

## Core principles
- Draw a card whenever you need input or momentum.
- Tables serve imagination; discard results that don't fit.
- Find connections between generated elements.
- Play one Traveler at a time; mechanics run the rest.
- When in doubt, go weird.

## Preparation
Steps 1–7 of the normal Journey prep (start, destination, route, number of Stops, personal Goals, personal Threats, vehicle). Step 8 — Threat Countdowns, Stops, Encounters — is generated during play to preserve surprise. Chapter 8 supplies a Destinations table, per-archetype Goal/Threat suggestions, a personal Threat table, and a '90s vehicle table.

**Internal Threats**: the core rules avoid them (they cost players agency), but solo play may use addiction, grief and other inward demons.

## Event triggers
Face cards fire events; suit selects the type. Do not reshuffle until the deck is exhausted — the deck is the pacing timer.

| Suit | Event |
|---|---|
| Spades | Personal Threat Countdown event |
| Clubs | Stop Countdown event |
| Hearts | Traveler event |
| Diamonds | Conversation |

Personal Threat Countdown (improvised to the Threat): 1 the Threat appears, 2 the conflict escalates or tilts, 3 it climaxes and resolves. Prep steps 5 and 6 are *each* Traveler's Goal and Threat, so the Countdown is per Traveler, not one clock for the party — a face card does not say whose, so it falls on whoever holds the spotlight.

## Tilts
Draw one card to judge whether an element helps or harms, and how much.
- **Clubs / Spades** — bad for the Travelers. **Hearts / Diamonds** — good.
- Degree by rank: 2–3 low, 4–6 medium, 7–9 high, 10–Ace extreme (ace highest). The transcript's table is de-interleaved and cannot settle the split on its own; the secondary summary corroborates this one.
- Tilts are optional and yield to anything already established.

## Generators
- **Stops** — roll the Chapter 5 setting/Blocker/conflict/mood tables at the moment of arrival.
- **Minor Encounters** — between Stops; a table of natures plus a Tilt for valence. Unlike Stops, they can be driven past.
- **NPCs** — draw for **motive** (Clubs narcissism, Spades trauma, Hearts community, Diamonds technology) and **method** (Clubs power, Spades deceit, Hearts religion, Diamonds technology); add a quirk; suit and rank also set importance and predisposition toward the Travelers.
- **Conversations** — subject D6: personal background, emotional state, religion, technology, the war, the Stop's background. The draw doubles as a Tilt on the outcome.
- **Traveler events** D6: 1 a Traveler confronts their Flaw, 2 a Threat catches up, 3 a Traveler pursues their Dream, 4 a conversation raises Tension, 5 a conversation lowers Tension, 6 a Traveler wanders off.

Tension between Travelers is tracked by judgment: bad conversations raise it, good ones reduce it.

## Mapping locations in solo play

No physical map. Mark the current location on paper, circle it, and draw lines to connected
locations as they are discovered — a mind map rather than a floor plan. Dotted lines mark
locations or NPCs merely heard about. Establish 3–4 locations visible from the Blocker to
open the Stop, then generate more from the p. 126 table as needed. Each location can be a scene.
