# Neurocasting, Drones, Vehicles & Gear

## The neurocaster
Three attributes, each a gear bonus for a different task, each degradable by pushing. Any attribute at 0 = **Busted**: repairs required, and if worn inside a neuroscape the user is disconnected — current Hope drops to 0 and a mental trauma is rolled.

- Wearing one: –2 dice to real-world actions requiring mobility or vision (–1 for the GO). You cannot act in both the real world and the neuroscape in the same round.
- Wired into a terminal: +2 dice to all neurocaster rolls.
- Access is location-dependent: global neuroscapes, local-only, or no access at all.

## Bliss
- +1 Bliss on **each failed roll inside a neuroscape**, counted before pushing. Only failing an opposed roll *as the active party* generates Bliss.
- Scripted experiences can inflict Bliss; Wits roll, each 6 cancels 1 point.
- **Bliss ≥ current Hope** → lost in the Electric State: you cannot disconnect voluntarily and will die of hunger/thirst unless someone removes the helmet — which zeroes current Hope and inflicts a mental trauma (same as being Busted or Incapacitated while connected). You can still act inside and keep accruing Bliss.
- Reduction: –1 per full day without neurocasting, but roll 1d6 per point — on a 1 that point becomes **Permanent Bliss**.
- Neuroresistant talent: one Wits roll to leave despite Bliss ≥ Hope.

## Tasks inside a neuroscape
Difficulty 1–3 = the number of separate successful rolls required. Each roll takes a Stretch.

- **Find information** — Wits + Processor. Data miner +2 dice. A failed roll pushes subsequent attempts at the same information to a Shift each. Sample Difficulty: city maps 1, common knowledge 1, building security 2, sensitive personal info 2, classified military 3, who controls the neuroscape 3.
- **Hack systems** — Wits + Network. Hacker +2 dice. Difficulty disables the system for a Stretch; +1 to seize control, +1 to extend to a Shift. Sample: consumer security or consumer drone 1, advanced security / police drone / local neuroscape 2, military-grade / military drone / global neuroscape 3+. Failure typically trips an alarm; retries stay at a Stretch each. Authorized systems (your own drone) need no roll.
- **Interact with avatars** — Empathy + Graphics (Gamer +2). Opposition rolls Wits + their Network. **Avatar manipulation** (rewriting someone's core beliefs) needs 2–4 successful Empathy rolls, one per Shift.
- **Avatar combat** — treated as close combat at Engaged range but rolling **Wits**, + Graphics gear dice, Gamer +2. Damage reduces the *user's* Health; at 0 the user is Incapacitated and disconnected, auto-rallies after a Stretch with 1 Health, and rolls mental trauma. Dual combat: choose real world or neuroscape each turn; you are inert in the other.
- Helpers on the same neuroscape give +1 die each and can do nothing else.
- **Intercerebral Intelligences**: native entities with Wits and Empathy (potentially superhuman), no neurocaster needed.

## Drones
- Controlled via neurocaster: use the drone's Strength and Agility, your own Wits and Empathy, plus Network as gear dice on every roll. Built-in weapons use Network instead of a gear bonus.
- **Hull** replaces Health; at 0 the operator is disconnected and the drone needs repair. Most have an Armor Level with no Agility penalty.
- Every failed roll while operating gives 1 Bliss.
- Powered by Vanadium Redox batteries (months of life).
- Consumer models: Kid Kosmo, Wally Wayne, Civilian Flyer (flight-capable), Johnny Jolt, Elite Trooper, Jury-Rigged. Military drones and drone ships are mostly wrecks. **Drone growths** act in both realms and can be attacked in both. **Robots** are drones without pilots, usually lacking Wits/Empathy.

## Vehicles
Stats: Passengers, Maneuverability, Speed, Hull, Armor, Cost. Journeys start with half a tank; a typical 20-gallon tank ≈ 400 miles. Ground vehicles only unless the Journey says otherwise.

Range spans horse/bicycle up to bus, boats, helicopter ($200,000), light airplane, small commercial drone ship; military drone ships are not commercially available.

D66 vehicle traits: Fast (+1 Speed), Roomy (+2 Passengers), Reliable (+2 repair dice), Slow, Heavy (+1 Hull), Powerful (+1 Maneuverability), Luxury model, Boneshaker, Light frame, Cheap model, Loud, Custom paintjob. Roll 1–2 traits at creation. Chase rules: transcript ~line 7500 (Ch 4, "Chases", p. 106).

## Gear and repairs
Gear bonuses erode via pushed 1s; at 0 the item is Busted. Repairs need a Wits roll and appropriate tools (general, vehicle, weapon, neurocaster). Hope-restoring items (dog, Walkman, books, liquor, instrument) cap at 1 Hope per Shift. **Neurine** is the setting's neuro-drug (p. 109).
