# The Traveler (PC)

## Creation sequence
1. Archetype (10 total; one per group). 2. Attributes. 3. Health/Hope. 4. Starting talent (+1 if total attributes ≤ 15). 5. Dream. 6. Flaw. 7. Favorite '90s song. 8. Description. 9. Name. 10. Neurocaster model. 11. Personal item. 12. Starting cash. 13. GM presents the Journey. 14. Note Destination, personal Goal, personal Threat. 15. Vehicle + 3 shared items. 16. Introductions and Tension.

## Attributes
Strength, Agility, Wits, Empathy — range 2–6.
- Roll: 4d6, re-roll 1s until all ≥ 2, assign freely.
- Or point-buy: distribute 16 points, none below 2 or above 6.
- Archetype names a key attribute. Total ≤ 15 → extra talent.

## Derived
- **Health** = (Strength + Agility) / 2, round up. +2 with the Tough talent.
- **Hope** = (Wits + Empathy) / 2, round up. +2 with the Dreamer talent.
- **Bliss** — neuronic addiction counter; see [04-neuronics.md](04-neuronics.md). Bliss ≥ current Hope = lost in the Electric State.

## Talents
~50 listed; each learnable once; most give +2 dice in a narrow situation. Notable non-+2 talents:
- **Dirty fighter** — unarmed base Damage 2.
- **Drama queen** — Tension bonus doubled in opposed rolls vs. Travelers.
- **Dreamer / Tough** — +2 max Hope / Health.
- **Intuition** — once per session, ask the GM for useful information.
- **Lone wolf** — reduce Tension without another Traveler present.
- **Medic** — Wits roll (action) to stabilize the Incapacitated.
- **Menacing** — threaten with Strength instead of Empathy, +2 dice.
- **Neuroresistant** — one Wits roll to leave a neuroscape despite Bliss ≥ Hope.
- **Nine lives** — death rolls with 6 dice instead of 4.
- **Nurse** — patients heal 2/Shift; capacity = Wits.
- **Surgeon** — may perform surgery (Wits) on injuries requiring it.
- **Techno babbler** — Wits instead of Empathy for technical persuasion.
Custom talents allowed with GM approval if they don't overlap existing ones.

## Dream, Flaw, song
- **Dream**: general aspiration, guides roleplay, fuels advancement.
- **Flaw**: core obstacle; may be overcome once, late in the Journey.
- **Favorite '90s song**: no mechanical effect; played in the car.

## Goal and Threat
Each Traveler gets a Journey-specific personal **Goal** (specific; should reflect the Dream; usually tied to the Destination) and a personal **Threat** opposing it, with a GM-authored 3-step Countdown the player never sees. Each Goal has a **Kicker** — the event that forced the Traveler onto the road.

## Gear
Neurocaster + one personal item + rolled starting cash, all by archetype. Group has a vehicle plus **3 shared items** (D66 table: tool sets, first aid kit, liquor, binoculars, canned food, dog, books, Walkman, painkillers, crowbar, tent, walkie-talkies, jerrycan, Vanadium Redox battery, weapons, consumer drones). No encumbrance tracking; GM may call for a Strength roll.

## Tension (0–2, asymmetric, per other Traveler)
- 0 nothing / 1 suppressed feeling / 2 uncontained emotion.
- Grants bonus dice equal to your rating in any opposed roll against that Traveler.
- Start: Tension 1 toward one or two others, 0 toward the rest.
- **Reducing**: talk it out in a scene with no immediate threat (≥ 1 Stretch, max two Travelers per scene) — both lower Tension by 1 and each regains 1 Hope. This is the primary Hope economy ("the Hope loop": raise Tension in play, discharge it in interpersonal scenes).
- Tension can be raised or lowered at any time, including between sessions; GM has final say. Tension triangles are encouraged.

## Advancement (debrief after each session)
- State how you followed your Dream and/or Flaw. If the table agrees, pick the attribute that best matches what you learned and roll 1d6: **higher than the current score** → +1 to that attribute (and any derived Health/Hope max, with equal current gain); **equal or lower** → gain a new talent, justified in fiction.
- **Overcoming your Flaw**: declare at debrief; if the table agrees, make 3 improvement rolls immediately and remove the Flaw. No further improvement afterward — do it with a session or two of play remaining.
- Reusing a Traveler in a new Journey requires a new Flaw.

## Archetypes
Artist, Criminal, Devotee, Doctor, Drone Pilot (a drone-bodied Traveler; "this archetype is a little different"), Investigator, Journalist, Outsider, Runaway Kid, Scientist, Veteran — each with key attribute, three suggested talents, Dream/Flaw tables, personal item and cash tables. Full entries: transcript lines 3838–4799. Four pre-generated Travelers appear at the end of the book.
