# Core Mechanics

## Resolution
- Roll d6 pool = relevant attribute (2–6) + talent/gear/situational modifiers. Minimum 1 die.
- Success = at least one **6**. Extra 6s = extra effect (+1 damage each in combat; speed/style/stealth/bonus effect otherwise, GM adjudicates).
- Other rolls used: D6, 2D6, D66 (pick a tens die and a ones die).

## Pushing
- On any roll you may push once: re-roll every die not showing 1 or 6.
- Each **1** on a *base* die after the push costs 1 current Hope.
- Each **1** on a *gear* die after the push reduces that gear's bonus by 1; at bonus 0 the gear is **Busted** and needs repair.
- NPCs never push.

## Gear dice
Separate colour. Weapons, tools, neurocaster attributes give gear bonuses. Gear degrades only via pushes.

## Helping
Up to three helpers, each +1 die, must plausibly assist. In combat, helping consumes the helper's turn.

## Opposed rolls
- Active party needs more 6s than the opponent. Only the active party may push.
- Traveler vs. Traveler: each side adds bonus dice equal to their **Tension** toward the other.
- Open opposed roll (both equally active): both may push; a tie forces a compromise, or re-roll if no compromise is possible.

## Time units
| Unit | Duration | Primary use |
|---|---|---|
| Round | 5–10 sec | Combat |
| Stretch | 5–10 min | Neurocasting |
| Shift | 5–10 hours | Travel (4 Shifts/day: Morning, Day, Evening, Night) |

## Six principles
1. Play to find out — no predetermined outcome.
2. The Travelers are seekers — driven by dreams, they engage.
3. The Journey is the goal — don't rush the Destination; resolve the Stop.
4. The world is falling apart — visible decay everywhere, escalating.
5. Neuronics are everywhere — no one escapes the network, not even Travelers.
6. It's the '90s, but not — 1997 nostalgia contrasted with alien neuro tech.

Tone targets: drama, horror, personal growth. Safety tools (lines/veils, safety cards, debrief) are recommended in the text.
