# Combat, Damage & Hazards

## Zones and range
Indoors a zone ≈ a room; outdoors ≈ 100 ft across.

| Range | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Engaged | In your face |
| Short | Same zone |
| Medium | Adjacent zone |
| Long | Up to four zones |
| Extreme | Further |

Attacking below a weapon's minimum range: –2 dice per band. Never usable beyond maximum range.

## Rounds
- The side that starts the fight acts first, in any order, then the defenders.
- Unclear initiator: each side rolls D6 + highest Wits on that side; higher total attacks first. Re-roll ties. Not an action, cannot be pushed.
- Turn = one move + one action, or two moves. Move precedes action.
- **Reaction** (fight back, dodge, etc.) forfeits your next turn but covers any number of incoming attacks until then.
- Free actions: drawing a weapon, opening a door, a few words.

## Attacks
- **Close combat** (Engaged, any weapon or none): roll Strength + weapon gear dice. Target chooses *take the hit* or *fight back* (opposed; only attacker may push).
- **Ranged** (Short+): roll Agility + weapon gear dice. Target chooses *stand tall* or *dodge* (opposed).
- Damage = weapon base Damage + 1 per extra 6 beyond what was needed.
- Fight back won by the target: attacker takes target's base Damage + extras.
- Firearms in close combat use Strength and usually eat the –2/band minimum-range penalty.
- Ammunition is not tracked. **Single-shot** weapons reload as an action. **Full auto**: on a hit you may immediately fire another burst (same or new target), and a third on another hit; empties the magazine (belt-fed excepted); reload is an action.
- **Ambush**: Agility roll; target cannot fight back or dodge. –3 dice to sneak into close combat. Impossible against someone already in active combat.

## Damage and death
- Health 0 = **Incapacitated**: may mumble and crawl one move/turn, no attribute rolls, no talents.
- **Death roll**: 4 dice each turn while Incapacitated, cannot be pushed. Accumulate 3 total 6s → stabilized. Three failed death rolls → dead. Nine lives talent → 6 dice.
- **Instant kill**: a single hit of ≥ 2× max Health.
- **Rallying** (Empathy roll, an action, same zone): restores Health equal to 6s rolled but does not stabilize. Unrallied, you rally alone after a Stretch and regain 1 Health.
- **Stabilizing**: Medic talent + Wits roll (first aid kit gives gear dice) ends death rolls; still Incapacitated.
- Additional damage while Incapacitated restarts death rolls.
- **Recovery**: 1 Health per Shift without strenuous activity; 2 with a Nurse. Blocked by disease, hunger or cold.
- **Serious injury**: roll D66 after surviving Incapacitation (11–36 = none). Effects range from broken finger to internal bleeding; several require surgery (Shift of time, Surgeon talent, Wits roll; failure re-Incapacitates the patient).
- Neuroscape damage: no death rolls; auto-rally after a Stretch with 1 Health, and roll mental trauma instead of physical injury.

## Hope
- Lost to pushed 1s and to traumatic events. Sample traumatic events: friend Incapacitated/killed 1, attacked by something horrible 1, contaminated 2, mutilated corpses 2, cornered by your worst fear 3, tortured 3.
- Traumatic event: Empathy roll, each 6 cancels 1 point of loss; pushing risks further loss. Losing ≥ 1 Hope to a traumatic event also makes you **freeze** (lose next turn).
- Hope 0 = **Breakdown**: can talk, move and flee but cannot roll attributes or use talents. Rally = someone else's Empathy roll (action), restoring Hope equal to 6s; otherwise self-rally after a Shift for 1 Hope.
- Hope recovery is otherwise only via reducing Tension (1 point) and Hope-restoring gear (max 1 per Shift). Nothing recovers Hope while hungry or sleep deprived.
- **Mental trauma**: D66 after a rallied Breakdown (11–36 = none). Effects include Apathetic (cannot push), Obsessive (must push every roll), Reclusive (no Hope from Tension), Phobic, Nightmares, Panic attacks, Psychotic, Amnesiac, Personality split. One Wits or Empathy roll per week to recover. The book flags this table for a group safety discussion.

## Other hazards
- **Explosions**: Blast Power dice, each 6 = 1 damage to everything at Short range of the impact; not pushable. Dodging = Agility, each 6 cancels 1. Explosive weapon attacks roll Agility; a miss lands in a random adjacent zone. Blast Power: molotov 6, grenade 8, rocket launcher 10, mortar 12, howitzer 14.
- **Fire**: Intensity dice (torch 4, burning furniture 6, burning room 8), each 6 = 1 damage, repeats while burning, +2 Intensity/round as it spreads; Agility opposed vs. Intensity to stop it.
- **Cold**: without shelter/clothing, Strength roll per Shift (per Stretch if extreme); failure = 1 damage and no natural healing until warmed.
- **Disease**: Virulence 4–10 (flu 4, pneumonia 6, ebola 10). Opposed Strength vs. Virulence daily; each excess 6 for the disease = 1 damage; no Health recovery while sick. Nurse + Stretch adds a Wits roll's 6s to yours.
- **Falling**: damage = metres/2 rounded down; controlled jump = Agility, each 6 cancels 1.
- **Hunger/thirst**: Strength roll per day without food or water, failure = 1 damage; no Health or Hope recovery while starving.
- **Sleep deprivation**: after 4 Shifts awake, no Hope recovery and a Wits roll each Shift or fall asleep for a Shift.
