# The Electric State Roleplaying Game — Complete Reference

---

## Chapter 1: Welcome to the Electric State

### Core Concepts
- **Setting**: Alternate 1997, after the Second Civil War (1975–1984).
- **Pacifica**: The nation formed from what was once California.
- **Neuronics**: Technology that interfaces with the human brain.
- **Neurocasters**: Helmet‑like devices used to access the Electric State.
- **The Journey**: The game focuses on the journey itself, not the destination.
- **Themes**: Drama, horror, personal growth, and societal decay.

### Principles of the Game
1. **Play To Find Out** – No predetermined plot; the dice and choices shape the story.
2. **The Travelers Are Seekers** – Characters are driven by dreams and hope.
3. **The Journey Is the Goal** – Interacting with people and events at Stops is central.
4. **The World Is Falling Apart** – Decay and collapse are omnipresent.
5. **Neuronics Are Everywhere** – Technology is inescapable.
6. **It’s the ’90s, but Not** – Blend '90s nostalgia with strange, advanced tech.

### Safety Tools
- Pre‑game discussions about lines and veils.
- Safety cards shown during play.
- Post‑game debriefings.
- Respect any player’s wish to raise a concern or leave the table.

### Dice & Rolling
- Uses standard six‑sided dice (D6).
- **Success**: At least one `6`.
- **Custom dice**: red base dice (❖ on 1 for Hope loss) and black gear dice (⚙ on 1 for gear damage).
- **Push**: Re‑roll all dice that are not 1 or 6.
- **Cost of Pushing**: Each `1` on base dice reduces Hope by 1; each `1` on gear dice reduces the gear bonus by 1.

### Time Units
| Unit | Duration | Primary Use |
|------|----------|-------------|
| **Round** | 5–10 seconds | Combat |
| **Stretch** | 5–10 minutes | Neurocasting |
| **Shift** | 5–10 hours | Travel, rest |

### Neuronics

**Drones** – remote‑controlled machines:

- Consumer drones: gaming, practical use.
- Powered by Vanadium Redox batteries.

- Require neurine and a vanadium redox battery.

- Can be local or global.
- Users appear as avatars.

- Founded by retired drone pilots.

## Chapter 3: Your Traveler

### Character Creation Steps
1. Choose Archetype (pages 66–75).
2. Roll Attributes (4d6, re‑roll 1s) and assign to Strength, Agility, Wits, Empathy.
3. Calculate Health = (Strength + Agility) ÷ 2 (round up).
4. Calculate Hope = (Wits + Empathy) ÷ 2 (round up).
5. Roll or choose starting talent (page 56).
6. If total attributes ≤ 15, gain an additional talent.
7. Roll or choose Dream (page 59).
8. Roll or choose Flaw (page 59).
9. Roll or choose favourite ’90s song (page 60).
10. Describe your Traveler.
11. Choose a name.
12. Roll or choose neurocaster model (page 61).
13. Roll or choose personal item (page 61).
14. Roll for starting cash (page 61).
15. Note Destination, personal Goal, and personal Threat.
16. Determine vehicle and shared items (page 62).
17. Set Tension levels with other Travelers (page 64).

### Health & Hope
- **Health**: reduces from damage. At 0 → Incapacitated (death rolls).
- **Hope**: reduces from pushing rolls and traumatic events. At 0 → Breakdown (mental trauma).
- **Maximum Health** = (Strength + Agility) ÷ 2 (round up), +2 if Tough talent.
- **Maximum Hope** = (Wits + Empathy) ÷ 2 (round up), +2 if Dreamer talent.

### Tension
- **0**: No tension.
- **1**: Suppressed irritation, love, or interest.
- **2**: Uncontained strong emotions (rage, love, fear).
- Asymmetrical: can differ between Travelers.

- Can be reduced (both reduce by 1) to regain 1 Hope.

- **Goal**: specific objective for the Journey.
- **Threat**: something/someone trying to harm or stop the Traveler.
- **Kicker**: event that forced the Traveler onto the road.

## Chapter 4: Combat & Neuronics

### Zones & Range
| Range | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Engaged** | In your face |
| **Short** | Same zone |
| **Medium** | Adjacent zone |
| **Long** | Up to 4 zones away |
| **Extreme** | Further away |

### Combat Round
- Each participant gets one turn per round.
- **Move**: move between adjacent zones (or from Short to Engaged).
- **Action**: attack, reload, take cover, rally, etc.
- **Free Actions**: drawing weapon, opening door, speaking.
- **Reaction**: Fight Back or Dodge; forfeits your next turn.

### Close Combat
- Roll **Strength** to hit.
- Target chooses: **Take the Hit** (takes damage) or **Fight Back** (opposed roll).
- If fighting back, attacker and target both roll Strength; more successes wins; loser takes damage equal to opponent's base Damage + extra successes.

### Ranged Combat
- Roll **Agility** to hit.
- Target chooses: **Stand Tall** (takes damage) or **Dodge** (opposed roll).
- If dodging, attacker and target both roll Agility; attacker needs more successes to hit; if fewer/equal, attack misses.
- **Cover**: roll cover's Armour Level dice, each `6` eliminates 1 damage.

### Ambush
- Target unaware: must take hit / stand tall, cannot fight back or dodge.
- To ambush, roll Agility; –3 dice to sneak up for close combat.

### Damage & Incapacitation
- Health at 0 → Incapacitated.
- **Death Rolls**: roll 4 dice per turn; need 3 successes total to stabilise. If 3 failures before that, dead.
- **Instant Kill**: damage ≥ twice max Health in one hit.
- **Rallying**: Empathy roll (action) to restore Health equal to successes; target can act again, but still needs death rolls until stabilised.
- **Stabilising**: Medic talent + Wits roll; stops death rolls.
- **Serious Injuries**: roll on D66 table when Incapacitated and surviving (page 84). Effects include broken limbs, concussion, internal bleeding, etc.
- **Surgery**: requires Surgeon talent; takes a Shift; failed surgery causes Incapacitation.

### Hope & Traumatic Events
- **Traumatic Event**: GM indicates potential Hope loss (1–3). Roll Empathy; each `6` reduces loss by 1.
- **Freeze**: if you lose Hope from a traumatic event, you lose your next turn.
- **Breakdown**: Hope at 0 → cannot roll attributes; can be rallied by Empathy roll.
- **Recovery**: reduce Tension to regain Hope; also some gear (Walkman, pet, book) can restore 1 Hope per Shift.

### Hazards
- **Explosions**: Blast Power dice; each `6` = 1 damage to all in zone. Can dodge with Agility (each `6` reduces damage by 1).
- **Fire**: Intensity 4–8; roll Intensity dice, each `6` = 1 damage. Fire spreads (+2 Intensity per round). Can roll Agility to stop spread.
- **Cold**: Strength roll per Shift (or Stretch in extreme); failure = 1 damage and cannot heal naturally.
- **Disease**: Virulence (4–10); opposed Strength vs. Virulence. Losses cause damage; daily rolls until cured.
- **Falling**: damage = height in meters ÷ 2 (rounded down). Agility roll reduces damage by 1 per success.
- **Hunger/Thirst**: Strength roll per day without food/water; failure = 1 damage; cannot recover Health or Hope.
- **Sleep Deprivation**: after 24h without sleep, cannot recover Hope; Wits roll per Shift or fall asleep.

**Bliss**:
- Every failed neurocasting roll (before pushing) gives +1 Bliss.
- If Bliss ≥ current Hope → you are lost in the Electric State. Cannot leave voluntarily.
- Forcible removal → Hope to 0 and mental trauma.
- **Reducing Bliss**: –1 per full day without neurocasting, but roll 1 die per point; on a 1, that point becomes permanent.

**Finding Information**:
- Difficulty 1–3 (number of successful Wits rolls). Processor adds gear bonus. Each roll takes a Stretch; failure takes a Shift for next attempt.

**Hacking Systems**:
- Base Difficulty 1–3. Network adds gear bonus. To take control or extend time to a Shift, Difficulty +1. Failure triggers an alarm.

**Avatar Combat**:
- Use Wits instead of Strength; Graphics gives gear bonus. Attacks reduce Health; at 0 Health, disconnected (no death rolls, auto‑rally after Stretch, but mental trauma).
